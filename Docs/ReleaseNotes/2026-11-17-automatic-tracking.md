# Automatic heart-rate recording and local daily Strain

The previous tracker path displayed incoming BPM. Only a manually started workout retained readings and calculated a session estimate, so leaving the band connected did not populate the daily Strain ring. This revision subscribes to timestamped Bluetooth readings independently of workout recording.

Received readings are processed automatically and saved in the existing protected local JSON store in 15-second batches, also flushing when the app leaves the foreground. The store uses complete-until-first-authentication file protection: data remains encrypted on the device and background writes can proceed after the first unlock following a reboot. An interrupted save retains its pending batch and reports an error with Retry Save in Device Settings. Daily recording metadata and one actual chart sample per UTC minute survive full backups and measurement JSON exports. No averages or missing readings are synthesized. Appending new automatic batches preserves imported scores, existing chart readings at other timestamps, workouts and other measurements. Manual workouts share the timestamped stream and do not add their session estimate to the daily estimate, avoiding double-counting.

The existing local Strain engine uses configured resting/maximum HR limits, a 30-reading smoothing look-ahead, and observed intervals capped at three seconds. It needs at least 32 fresh readings before the first score. Default HR limits (55/190 BPM) are assumptions, editable in Device Settings; changing them resets smoothing for new readings without rewriting prior load. Day changes and gaps of at least 30 seconds start a new segment. Pending smoothing intervals, absent delivery and disconnected periods are not billed. The estimate is separate from the provider's score, labeled LOCAL STRAIN ESTIMATE on Home, details and Live. An imported WHOOP Strain score takes display priority. Local estimates do not become official-score export highlights.

Home and Device Settings offer a shortcut to today when reviewing an older date. Device Settings shows received reading count, counted minutes, last saved reading, the estimate and save failures. Existing Bluetooth background mode is retained; the central now opts into state restoration and makes one reconnect attempt after unexpected loss. iOS controls background execution and delivery. Force-quitting, an unsupported stream or a disconnected phone can leave gaps. This does not implement strap-history backfill or establish all-day physical-device reliability.

Recovery, Sleep, HRV and other overnight/provider measurements are not derived from a BPM broadcast. Home explains this and opens the existing WHOOP account flow; supported ZIP/JSON imports remain available. No proprietary Recovery algorithm or fictitious scores are added. Full official-app functionality remains incomplete.
