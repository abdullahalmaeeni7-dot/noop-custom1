# Actual native review: iteration 1

These images were captured by the actual Release app in an isolated iPhone 15 Pro simulator using Xcode 26.3. The Home image uses synthetic measurements seeded only into this disposable simulator; the empty first-launch image confirms onboarding. They are not screenshots of the browser reconstruction.

Compared visually against the supplied screenshots and existing measured tokens. Visible differences addressed in the next iteration: Home used a plain house; Health used a square-card symbol; Community showed two people; the streak used a monochrome flame; the Day in Review bar was about 56 points instead of 44; the main background had extra colored gradients; custom glass edges were too prominent. The next source reuses a house/chart outline, heart/pulse and three-person symbols, uses the flame emoji, reduces the review height, and removes the extra Home gradients and strong custom edges.

Data-dependent differences remain intentional: no fake battery reading, medical baseline, stress score, proprietary age/aging output or unsupported service data. Nutrition remains in the navigation as requested. The local day review reports actual saved measurements; camera capture does not imply an AI service is configured. Exact pixel/animation parity and physical camera behavior are not established by these static images.
